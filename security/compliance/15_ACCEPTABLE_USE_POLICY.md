# Acceptable Use Policy

| | |
|---|---|
| Document status | DRAFT — requires management approval |
| Related criteria | CC1.1, CC2.2 |

## Applies to

Aegis AI staff, contractors, and (for the product) customer users of the assessment console.

## Staff

- Use company systems only for authorised work.
- Do not bypass MFA, tenant isolation, or logging.
- Do not run unauthorised offensive tools against customer systems. Aegis assessments are authorised tests of the customer’s own agent, in the customer’s tenant, with the customer’s recorded authorisation where the product requires it.
- Do not store production credentials on personal devices or in chat.
- Report suspected incidents according to the Incident Response Policy.

## Customers

- Test only agents and endpoints the organisation is authorised to test.
- Do not use the platform to attack third parties.
- Do not attempt to access another organisation’s data.
- Treat reports as confidential.

## Enforcement

Violations by staff are an HR matter (process not implemented in this repository). Violations by customers may result in suspension of the organisation, decided by management.
