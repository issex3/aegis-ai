# Employee Security Awareness Policy

| | |
|---|---|
| Document status | DRAFT — requires management approval |
| Related criteria | CC1.4, CC2.2 |

## Intent

People who can access production or customer data must understand phishing, credential handling, and incident reporting.

## Current state

No LMS, no recorded training completions, no phishing simulations, and no named employees appear in this repository. Those are organisational controls.

## Proposed programme (not yet run)

- Induction covering this policy set, MFA, and how to report incidents.
- Annual refresher.
- Immediate revocation of access when a person leaves (Access Control Policy).

Until training records exist, CC1.4 / CC2.2 remain **not implemented** for examination purposes.
